# Short-form channels

## WhatsApp

- Headline: brief broadcast title (max ~40 chars)
- Body: 1–2 plain sentences
- Disclaimer: required disclaimer verbatim (may truncate layout only, not wording)
- CTA: short action (e.g. View details)

## Landing

- Headline: hero title (max ~70 chars)
- Body: informative subtext under hero
- Disclaimer: required disclaimer below fold area in copy
- CTA: primary hero button label
