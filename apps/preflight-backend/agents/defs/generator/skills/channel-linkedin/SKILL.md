# LinkedIn channel format

- Headline: professional hook (max ~120 chars)
- Body: one short paragraph; credible tone, no sales pressure
- Disclaimer: one line with required disclaimer verbatim
- CTA: link-style phrase (e.g. Read the offer document)
- Layout: card with headline, body, disclaimer line, CTA link
