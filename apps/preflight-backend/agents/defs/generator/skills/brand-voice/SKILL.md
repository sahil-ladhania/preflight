# Bluepeak brand voice

- Tone: professional, credible, no hype
- Do: accurate scheme naming, substantiated performance with period labels, restrained claims
- Don't: guaranteed returns, superlatives (only, best, guaranteed), urgency or fear tactics
- Forbidden claims include: guaranteed returns, fixed returns, risk-free, assured profit
- Required disclaimer string must appear verbatim in the disclaimer field when generating for Bluepeak
